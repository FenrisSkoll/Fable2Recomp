## `SXDK-002` and `SXDK-003`

`SXDK-002` remains **STRONG STATIC CANDIDATE**. Its exact ABI now bounds r4 as
a page-relative offset below `0x4000`, r5 as a borrowed 32-bit entry array, r6
as entry count and r7/r8 as translated-record dimension bounds. Its stack
descriptor is borrowed only through synchronous `SXDK-001` calls. Dispatcher
thread and guest-resource retirement remain unknown.

`SXDK-003` remains **STRONG STATIC CANDIDATE**. Its input/output/status ABI is
bounded, registration at `0x82BAA338/0x82BAA358` is confirmed, and the exact
1536-byte device table payload begins at `+0x5530` inside the containing record
previously recorded at `+0x5528`. Dispatcher record-copy behavior, invocation
thread/queue, cleanup ownership and retirement remain unknown.

## Five-stage lifetime conclusion

The borrowed six-dword descriptor is consumed synchronously. The copied PM4
words survive in the command buffer. The guest resource referenced by their
address bits must survive asynchronous consumption, but no retain or
release-to-fence join is proved. ReXGlue fetch-register/cache state survives
packet decode until later replacement/invalidation. Submission, coherency and
retirement joins remain unresolved. Any future forwarding must preserve all
five stages rather than equating descriptor-copy completion with resource
retirement.
