only to test joins and must never appear as Fable rendering evidence.

After the one prepared manual run, review at most one ordinary indexed/textured
draw from a complete consumer interval. The parser nominates at most one
further-evidence target with selected shaders, compatible non-null ordinary
texture views, no observed query/memexport indicator, native invocation and
submission evidence. This nomination is not final qualification and does not
establish payload correctness, input closure or renderer equivalence.

Record exact run/interval/decision, shader hashes and specializations, known
constant/geometry requirements, used view metadata, attachment/depth/output
state, nearby resolves and actual submission/completion edges. Inspect aliases,
special geometry/tessellation, resource validity and prior attachment contents.

details or GPU-written inputs may leave the candidate unsuitable even for the
first translation proof. Prefer the smallest missing observation over a larger
recorder. Do not choose a specialized copy because SXDK-001 is already known.

functional gameplay, visual correctness and recorder disturbance. Current
metadata lacks shader/resource/geometry/constant payloads and initial target
contents. No producer/material/character identification is established. A dog
visible in the user's view does not name any draw in the capture.
