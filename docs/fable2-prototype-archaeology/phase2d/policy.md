# Independent mapping review policy v1

Phase 2D is an offline adoption-readiness review of frozen Phase 2C. Recommendations
are not human decisions, canonical mappings, names or runtime changes. All human
decision fields start pending. There is no minimum recommendation count.

Every input is validated against the frozen commit and source graph before analysis.
The six inherited blocker records are copied verbatim from the frozen validation
envelope. They remain blocked; review does not reopen historical reconstruction.

The independent extractor reuses only the source-bound image loader and bounded
PowerPC instruction interpreter. It independently constructs canonical signatures
from complete raw bodies and consumed references. It does not read saved XREFs,
candidate profiles, grades or semantic results to decide a candidate. The complete
46,179 donor and 46,180 TU1 .pdata populations participate in reciprocal matching.

Canonicalization retains all instruction bits except branch displacements and the
low halves of proven two-instruction lis/addi or lis/ori address definitions. Exact
register and consumer roles, complete terminated text identity, encoding, terminator
and interior offset remain in the signature. Signed-low carry is recomputed. Empty
storage qualifies only at an explicit byte-read use. Unknown windows do not become
objects. The shared interpreter's bounded single-block coverage is a limitation.

The blinded pass accepts only donor identities and raw profile evidence. Donor
selection and raw literals are unavoidable disclosed inputs. Prior proposed targets,
grades and transported semantic labels are excluded from matching. This is an
engineering control, not psychological independence. The full candidate and reverse
sets are frozen on disk before target reveal or adoption simulation.

Mandatory validity gates are conjunctive: complete reference identity and definition/
use proof, exact .pdata boundaries and size, compatible CFG and instruction roles,
reciprocal uniqueness, injectivity and resolution of every call or external transfer.
No aggregate score compensates for a missing gate. Reference evidence consumed by
canonicalization supplies no independent corroborating vote. A mapped callee and an
import/helper at the same callsite are one obligation.

Independent corroboration can come from retained Phase 2A mapped callees, mapped
callers at corresponding offsets, or two-sided exact-delta neighbours. Suppressed
seeds are unavailable. Same-generation and circular dependencies are prohibited.
No semantic result may feed back into correspondence review. Internal reachable
regions can discharge a bounded call obligation when exact control flow and bytes
agree; they remain non-functions and carry a reservation.

Helper distinctiveness is measured within structurally compatible callers, with
full-population fan-in reported separately. Multiple same-role users indicate common
support. Single-support proposals require an explicit reservation or hold; a generic
literal or common helper by itself cannot establish a recommendation. Multiple
reference identities strengthen discrimination but are not multiple independent
support classes. Risk populations overlap and are reported as sets.

Review results are exactly one of recommend-human-approval,
recommend-human-approval-with-reservation, hold-for-additional-evidence,
recommend-downgrade or recommend-rejection. A disagreement with the frozen grade
is recorded alongside it, never by overwriting Phase 2C. Probable proposals can be
recommended only after completing the same reconstruction, blind, adversarial and
injectivity path. Spelling supplies no physics-wrapper exception.

Simulation removes the three semantic-transport suppressions before adding any
recommended proposals. It cannot restore a suppressed pair through dependencies,
September routing or a downstream view. It remains unused by production consumers.
Human approval requires separate user-supplied decision evidence, timestamp,
approver, exact proposal-set hash and batch identity. This task supplies none.
